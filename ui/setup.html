Welcome to Draftmeter

A small overlay for Zwift that shows what the game doesn't:

  • Draft gauge: how many watts you're saving in the slipstream, live.
  • Average draft: how well you sat in for the whole ride.
  • Gaps: riders around you, seconds ahead and behind.
  • Climb ahead: the next climb, its length and steepness.
  • Rivals' battery: an estimate of how much each rider near you has left.

How it works

Zwift already calculates all of this. Draftmeter signs in a second, free Zwift account that simply spectates you (like watching a friend in Zwift) and shows the numbers it receives. It works whatever you ride Zwift on (computer, Apple TV, iPad, phone), as long as this computer is online. Your normal Zwift password is never asked for.

Be aware: Draftmeter is a hobby project, not made by or affiliated with Zwift. It uses Zwift's unofficial spectator connection, which Zwift's terms don't officially allow. Your own account is never logged in by Draftmeter; the second account is what connects. Use it at your own risk.

1 · Create a second Zwift account

This "monitor" account only watches you. It must be different from the account you ride with.

  1. Open zwift.com/sign-up (opens your browser).
  2. Use a different email. Tip: a Gmail alias works, e.g. yourname+zwiftmon@gmail.com (mail still arrives in your normal inbox).
  3. Only fill in the first form (name, email, password). You can close the page after that. No subscription, trial or payment is needed; ignore those screens.

Then log in with it here

2 · Who do you ride as?

Search for the name of your normal Zwift account and click yourself.

    Can't find yourself?

    Try only your last name, or paste your athlete ID below. You can find it on ZwiftPower: open your profile, and the number after z= in the address bar is your ID (e.g. profile.php?z=1234567).

    You're set 🎉

    1. Start riding in Zwift. The overlay goes live a few seconds after you start pedalling (the first time it can take up to a minute while it connects).
    2. Place the overlay: drag it where you want it and resize it from the edges, then press ⌘⇧D to lock it, so clicks go through to Zwift.
    3. Draftmeter has a Dock icon (right-click it to quit) and a gauge icon in the menu bar with all options: panels, settings, quit.

    Tip: if Zwift is full screen and the overlay hides behind it, switch Zwift to a windowed or borderless window.
