DRAFTMETER for Windows: install in 1 minute
===========================================

1. Double-click "Draftmeter-Setup.exe".

2. Windows will warn "Windows protected your PC", because Draftmeter is a free
   hobby app without a paid code-signing certificate:
     - Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   It installs and starts by itself.

3. Draftmeter lives in the SYSTEM TRAY (bottom right, maybe hidden under the
   ^ arrow; a small gauge icon). Quit it there: right-click > Quit.
   A setup window walks you through:
     - creating a second, free Zwift account (takes 2 minutes)
     - finding yourself
   Your normal Zwift password is never asked for.

4. Ride in Zwift. The overlay fills in a few seconds after you start pedalling.
   Put Zwift in windowed or borderless mode if the overlay hides behind it.
   Ctrl+Shift+D locks/unlocks the overlay, Ctrl+Shift+H hides it.

Note: Draftmeter is not made by or affiliated with Zwift. It uses Zwift's
unofficial spectator connection. Use at your own risk.
